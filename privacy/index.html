Legal · Privacy policy

We don’t collect your data.

That isn’t a promise we’re stretching to make — it’s a description of how the software is built. There is no server to send anything to. Here is the whole picture, in plain English.

Effective 28 July 2026

The short version

  • Our apps run entirely on your device. No accounts, no sign-in, no servers of ours, no analytics, no crash-reporting SDKs, no advertising, no tracking.
  • This website sets no cookies and runs no analytics. Even the fonts are served from our own domain, so no third party learns you visited.
  • If you email us, we see your email address and whatever you wrote. That is the only personal data we ever hold about anyone.

1. Who we are

This site and the apps described on it are published by Cloudberrix OÜ, a private limited company registered in Estonia (registry code 17557468), registered address Tartu mnt 67/1-13b, Kesklinna linnaosa, Tallinn, 10115, Harju maakond, Estonia. Where this policy describes personal data, Cloudberrix OÜ is the data controller for the purposes of the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

You can reach us at any time at support@cloudberrix.com. There is no separate privacy department — the same people who build the software read that inbox.

2. Our apps

Our apps are built to work without us. They do their job on your device, and that is where everything you put into them stays.

Nothing is sent to us

Our apps contain no analytics, no telemetry, no crash-reporting services, no advertising frameworks, and no third-party code that reports back. We do not operate servers that receive your content, and we could not read your data if we wanted to.

Where your information lives

Whatever you enter — settings, saved items, documents an app creates — is stored in that app’s own storage on your device. It is included in your device or iCloud backups if you have those switched on, which is a matter between you and Apple under Apple’s privacy policy; those backups are not accessible to us. Deleting the app removes its data from the device.

Permissions

If an app asks for access to something — the camera, for instance — it is used for the task you asked for, at the moment you asked for it, and nothing is retained or transmitted as a result. iOS will always ask you first, and you can withdraw any permission in Settings at any time.

Anything you share, you share deliberately

Some apps let you export or share a file. That happens only when you choose it, and it goes to the destination you pick. We are not a party to it and receive no copy.

Purchases

Apps and any in-app purchases are sold through Apple’s App Store. Apple handles the entire transaction and is the merchant of record. We never see your name, billing address, or payment details. Apple provides us with aggregated, anonymised sales and download reports that cannot be traced to an individual. Apple’s handling of your data is governed by Apple’s own privacy policy.

App Store privacy labels

Every app on the App Store carries Apple’s privacy label. Ours read “Data Not Collected”, and the app bundles include a privacy manifest declaring no collected data types and no tracking. If we ever build an app that genuinely needs to process something — a product with sync or an account, say — we will describe it on this page and in that app’s App Store label before it ships, not after.

3. This website

cloudberrix.com is a set of static pages. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics or tracking scripts, embeds no third-party widgets, and has no forms to fill in. Typefaces are self-hosted rather than loaded from a font network, specifically so that no third party is told you were here. There is no consent banner because there is nothing to consent to.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., a Microsoft company). Like any web server, that infrastructure necessarily processes your IP address, the page requested, and basic technical details in order to deliver the page and to defend against abuse. This is done by our host as part of serving the site; we have no access to those logs and build no profiles from them. This processing rests on our legitimate interest in operating a secure, functioning website (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)), and may involve servers outside the European Economic Area under the safeguards our host maintains.

4. If you write to us

When you email support@cloudberrix.com we receive your email address and whatever you choose to put in the message. We use it to answer you and, if it is a bug report, to fix the problem.

  • Lawful basis: our legitimate interest in responding to people who contact us, and in supporting our products (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)); or the steps necessary to perform a contract where your message concerns a purchase.
  • Retention: we keep correspondence only as long as it is useful — typically while the issue is open, and then no longer than two years so that we can recognise a returning conversation. After that it is deleted.
  • Never: we do not add you to a mailing list, use your address for marketing, sell it, or share it with anyone, other than the email provider that carries the message for us.

Please don’t send us sensitive personal information. We don’t need it, and email is not a secure channel for it.

5. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to ask for access to the personal data we hold about you, to have it corrected or erased, to restrict or object to how we use it, and to receive it in a portable form. Because the only thing we ever hold is correspondence you sent us, these requests are usually resolved the same week.

Email support@cloudberrix.com and we will answer within one month, as the regulation requires. Exercising these rights is free.

If you believe we have handled your data improperly, you are entitled to complain to a supervisory authority — in our case the Estonian Data Protection Inspectorate (Andmekaitse Inspektsioon, aki.ee), or the authority in your own country of residence. We would rather you told us first so we can put it right.

6. Children

Our apps are not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone — children included — because we do not collect personal data through our apps at all.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes we will update the effective date at the top of the page. If a change is material — if a future product processes data in a way this page does not already describe — we will say so clearly here, and the relevant App Store privacy label will reflect it before that product is released.
