The first one is nearly ripe.

Our first release is a mobile app — designed and built in Tallinn, and now deep in final testing. We’re keeping the name to ourselves a little longer: no teasers, no waiting list, no countdown. When it’s ready, it will simply be here.

It won’t be the last. Every Cloudberrix product joins the register below — different apps, for different industries, sharing one standard of finish.

Made for daylight.

Software gets used out in the world — in full sun, on the move, with one free hand and half a minute to spare. We design for that world: interfaces that stay legible on a bright street, answer at a glance, and hand you back your day. Screens made to be looked at briefly — then looked up from.

The studio · Tallinn, Estonia

Named after a berry you can’t farm.

Cloudberries grow one berry to a stalk, in the bogs of the north, and they ripen from copper to gold. You can’t plant them in rows — you walk the bog and pick them by hand.

We think software is worth making the same way: by hand, with patience, one product at a time until it’s ripe. Not one kind of app, and not one industry — wherever a piece of software deserves to be made properly, we’re interested. The register has room.
