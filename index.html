How we work

We make all of it ourselves
No templates, no bought-in parts. Every screen gets drawn, which is slower and a lot more enjoyable.
You pay once
One price, shown before you buy. No subscription, no upsell, nothing that renews while you’re not looking.
It’s just us
No investors, no board, no growth targets. The people who buy our apps are the entire business model.
We don’t disappear after launch
Shipping is where the job starts. We keep fixing things for as long as people are using them.

About

A small studio in Tallinn.

We build software because we love building it. That’s most of the reason this exists.

We’re not trying to grow into something big. We’d like to make a few good apps, look after them properly, and still enjoy the work in ten years.

That’s about it, really.
