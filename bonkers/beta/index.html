How to get it

  1. Get TestFlight. It's Apple's free app for trying games before they come out. Here it is on the App Store.
  2. Open the link. Tap Join the beta on your iPhone, then Accept.
  3. Install. BONKERS shows up on your home screen with an orange dot next to the name. That just means it's a beta.

Before you play

  • It's not finished, so expect bugs and the odd crash. Stuff will change from one build to the next.
  • Everything's unlocked for free in the beta. The final game may work differently.
  • You need an iPhone on iOS 17 or newer.
  • Each build runs out after 90 days. TestFlight will tell you when there's a new one.
  • Leaderboard scores from the beta might get wiped before launch.
  • There's a cap on testers. If the link says the beta is full, that's why.
  • Had enough? Delete the app, or tap Stop Testing in TestFlight.

Privacy

The game itself doesn't collect anything about you. There are no ads, no analytics and no sign-up. Your progress is saved on your phone, and in your iCloud if you have that switched on.

TestFlight is Apple's. Because you joined with a public link, we can't see your name or email. Apple does send us crash reports, install numbers and any feedback you send, and feedback can include your name and email.

We only use this to fix the game and never pass it on. If you'd like something deleted, email support@cloudberrix.com.

Our privacy notice has the whole picture, and the terms say what the beta does and doesn't promise. Here's Apple's TestFlight privacy page.
