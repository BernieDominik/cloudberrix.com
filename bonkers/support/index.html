What is free, and what THE LICENCE opens

THE FIELD is free to play, with the whole workshop. THE LICENCE is a one-time purchase that opens the six other levels, ENDLESS, and the two ships you earn in them. There are no ads and no subscription.

Getting THE LICENCE back

Tap RESTORE PURCHASES in SETTINGS › ACCOUNT, or RESTORE on THE LICENCE page, on a phone signed in to the Apple Account that bought it.

Moving to a new phone

Sign in to the same Apple Account with iCloud on, and leave CLOUD SAVE on in SETTINGS › ACCOUNT: your progress follows you. If THE LICENCE has not come back on its own, restore it as above.

Steering

Drag anywhere on the screen and pull; lift your thumb and the ship flies on. The weapons fire on their own. To steer by leaning the phone instead, pick TILT in SETTINGS › STEERING. If the ship drifts, RECENTRE TILT in the pause menu reads your neutral pose again.

Game Center

Leaderboards and achievements live on Game Center. Sign in from GAME CENTER in SETTINGS › ACCOUNT. It is optional, and the game plays the same without it.

Replaying the lesson

Tap REPLAY FLIGHT SCHOOL in SETTINGS › HELP. THE BADDIES, on the same page and on the menu, shows every baddie you’ve met and how to beat them.

Sound and comfort

MUSIC and SOUNDS are separate switches in SETTINGS › GENERAL. Screen shake, flashes and motion effects have their own switches there too.

The beta

The beta runs through Apple’s TestFlight, and the whole game is open in it for free. To tell us something, send feedback from the TestFlight app, take a screenshot while you play and share it as beta feedback, or email us. If you hit a bug, SEND YOUR SESSION LOG in SETTINGS › HELP helps us find it. The terms say what the beta does and doesn’t promise.

Privacy and terms

BONKERS collects nothing: no account, no analytics, no ads, no tracking. The privacy notice has the details, and the terms cover THE LICENCE, gold, the beta and fair play.
