BONKERS · Privacy notice

The game collects nothing.

BONKERS keeps your progress on your phone and in your own iCloud, and sends nothing to us. Here is the whole picture anyway, in plain English.

Effective 26 September 2026

The short version

  • No account, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, no tracking, and no servers of ours. The game sends nothing to us.
  • Your progress stays on your phone and, if you use iCloud, in your own iCloud. Game Center and purchases are handled by Apple.
  • If you email us, we see your address and whatever you wrote. That is the only personal data we ever hold.

Who we are

BONKERS is made by Cloudberrix OÜ, a private limited company registered in Estonia (registry code 17557468), at Tartu mnt 67/1-13b, Kesklinna linnaosa, Tallinn, 10115, Harju maakond, Estonia. Where this notice describes personal data, we are the data controller under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

You can write to us at support@cloudberrix.com. The people who make the game read that inbox.

What the game collects

Nothing. BONKERS has no account, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising and no tracking, and it contains no third-party code that reports back. It does not use your location, your contacts, your camera or your microphone. We run no server for it, and it sends nothing to us. The app declares the same to Apple: no data collected, and no tracking.

Three things happen only when you ask for them:

  • Links. The game opens a web page only when you tap one of its links to this site, such as the privacy policy in SETTINGS › ACCOUNT. The page opens in your browser, under this site’s own privacy policy.
  • Postcards. If you share a postcard from the game, the iOS share sheet sends it wherever you choose, and we receive no copy.
  • Tilt. If you steer by tilting the phone, the game reads the motion sensor to fly the ship. The readings are used on the phone and sent nowhere.

Your progress

Your progress, gold, settings, and any name you type on the THIS PHONE scoreboard are kept in the app’s own storage on your phone. We cannot see them. If iCloud Backup is on, your phone’s backup may include them, which is between you and Apple.

If you are signed in to iCloud, the game also keeps a copy of that save in your own iCloud key-value storage, so that another iPhone signed in to the same Apple Account can carry on where you left off. That copy belongs to your Apple Account and is held by Apple under Apple’s privacy policy; we have no access to it.

Game Center

Game Center is Apple’s service, and using it is optional. If you are signed in to Game Center on your phone, or sign in when the game offers, BONKERS sends your scores and achievements to Apple under your Game Center identity, and your scores can appear on leaderboards that other players see, under the nickname you chose in Game Center. That data is Apple’s to hold, under Apple’s privacy policy. If you are not signed in, the game sends nothing to Game Center and plays the same.

Purchases

THE LICENCE is sold through Apple’s App Store. Apple takes the payment as the merchant of record, as an independent controller under its own privacy policy, so we never see your name, your card or your Apple Account. Apple gives us sales reports that cannot be traced to an individual.

To tie a purchase to this copy of the game, the app gives Apple a random number that it made on your phone the first time it opened (StoreKit’s “app account token”). It says nothing about who you are, and we run no server that receives it. Restoring a purchase asks Apple which purchases your Apple Account already holds.

If you write to us

When you email support@cloudberrix.com we receive your email address, what you write, and any device or game detail you include. We use it to answer you, or to fix what you reported, and for nothing else. The lawful basis is our legitimate interest in supporting the game (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)), or performance of a contract where your message concerns a purchase.

We keep correspondence while the issue is open and no longer than two years after it is closed, then delete it. We do not add you to a mailing list, market to you, sell your address, or share it with anyone beyond the email provider that carries the message.

Children

BONKERS is made for a general audience. It collects no personal data from anyone who plays it, children included. Purchases go through Apple, where a parent who uses Ask to Buy in Family Sharing approves each one first.

Deleting your data

  • On your phone. Deleting the app deletes everything it kept on the phone.
  • In iCloud. Turn CLOUD SAVE off in SETTINGS › ACCOUNT, or turn iCloud off for BONKERS in the Settings app, and the game stops writing to your iCloud. The copy already there holds only your progress, settings and scoreboard names, and only BONKERS, on a phone signed in to your Apple Account, can read it.
  • Game Center. Sign out, or choose who can see your profile, in the Settings app under Game Center. Your Game Center data is held by Apple, and requests about it go to Apple, for example through privacy.apple.com.
  • Your emails to us. Ask at support@cloudberrix.com and we will delete them.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask for access to the personal data we hold about you, have it corrected or erased, restrict or object to how we use it, and receive it in a portable form. Since the only thing we ever hold is correspondence you sent us, these requests are usually resolved the same week. Email support@cloudberrix.com and we will answer within one month, as the regulation requires. Exercising these rights is free.

If you believe we have handled your data improperly you may complain to a supervisory authority: in our case the Estonian Data Protection Inspectorate (aki.ee), or the authority where you live. We would rather you told us first so we can put it right.

Changes

If this notice changes we will update the effective date above. If a future version of BONKERS processes data in a way this page does not already describe, we will say so here, and its App Store privacy label will reflect it, before that version ships.
