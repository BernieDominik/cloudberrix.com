BONKERS · Privacy notice

The game collects nothing.

BONKERS keeps your progress on your phone and in your own iCloud, and sends nothing to us. Here is the whole picture anyway, in plain English.

Effective 23 October 2026

The short version

  • No account, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, no tracking, and no servers of ours. The game sends nothing to us.
  • Your progress stays on your phone and, if you use iCloud, in your own iCloud. Game Center and purchases are handled by Apple.
  • If you email us, ask for beta or launch news, or send feedback from the beta, we see your email address and what you sent. That is the only personal data we hold, and we never sell it.

Who we are

BONKERS is made by Cloudberrix OÜ, a private limited company registered in Estonia (registry code 17557468), at Tartu mnt 67/1-13b, Kesklinna linnaosa, Tallinn, 10115, Harju maakond, Estonia. Where this notice describes personal data, we are the data controller under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

You can write to us at support@cloudberrix.com. The people who make the game read that inbox.

What the game collects

Nothing. BONKERS has no account, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising and no tracking, and it contains no third-party code that reports back. It does not use your location, your contacts, your camera or your microphone. We run no server for it, and it sends nothing to us. The app declares the same to Apple: no data collected, and no tracking.

Three things happen only when you ask for them:

  • Links. The game opens a web page only when you tap one of its links to this site, such as the privacy policy in SETTINGS › ACCOUNT. The page opens in your browser, under this site’s own privacy policy.
  • Postcards. If you share a postcard from the game, the iOS share sheet sends it wherever you choose, and we receive no copy. If you save one to Photos, iOS first asks whether BONKERS may add photos. It can only add them: it never sees your library.
  • Tilt. If you steer by tilting the phone, the game reads the motion sensor to fly the ship. The readings are used on the phone and sent nowhere.

Apple’s own reports. If you have chosen to share with app developers in your iPhone’s Settings (Privacy & Security › Analytics & Improvements), Apple sends us crash reports and figures about how the app runs. Apple also gives us App Store figures, such as how many people downloaded or opened the game. These come from Apple, not from the game, and none of them tells us who you are. You can stop sharing in that Settings screen at any time.

Your progress

Your progress, gold, settings, and any name you type on the THIS PHONE scoreboard are kept in the app’s own storage on your phone. We cannot see them. If iCloud Backup is on, your phone’s backup may include them, which is between you and Apple.

If you are signed in to iCloud, the game also keeps a copy of that save in your own iCloud key-value storage, so that another iPhone signed in to the same Apple Account can carry on where you left off. That copy belongs to your Apple Account and is held by Apple under Apple’s privacy policy; we have no access to it.

Game Center

Game Center is Apple’s service, and using it is optional. If you are signed in to Game Center on your phone, or sign in when the game offers, BONKERS sends your scores and achievements to Apple under your Game Center identity, and your scores can appear on leaderboards that other players see, under the nickname you chose in Game Center. That data is Apple’s to hold, under Apple’s privacy policy. If you are not signed in, the game sends nothing to Game Center and plays the same.

Purchases

THE LICENCE is sold through Apple’s App Store. Apple takes the payment as the merchant of record, as an independent controller under its own privacy policy, so we never see your name, your card or your Apple Account. Apple gives us sales reports that cannot be traced to an individual.

To tie a purchase to this copy of the game, the app gives Apple a random number that it made on your phone the first time it opened (StoreKit’s “app account token”). It says nothing about who you are, and we run no server that receives it. Restoring a purchase asks Apple which purchases your Apple Account already holds.

Testing the beta

The BONKERS beta runs through Apple’s TestFlight, under Apple’s TestFlight privacy notice. The beta game collects nothing more than the released one. Apple, not the game, passes some information to us:

  • Who you are. If you joined with our public link, Apple does not show us your name or email address. If we invited you by email, we see the name and address we invited.
  • Crashes and use. Crash reports, how many times the beta was installed and opened, and your phone’s model, iOS version, language and a device identifier. Apple keeps your phone’s name to itself.
  • Feedback. When you send feedback or a screenshot through TestFlight, Apple passes on what you wrote, the screenshot, and the state of the phone at the time (such as battery level, free space, connection and screen size). Apple says feedback may carry your name and email address.
  • Session log. The beta keeps a log of your runs on the phone: the level, difficulty, length and gold of each run, the screens you opened, and your phone’s model and iOS version. It reaches us only if you send it from SETTINGS › HELP › SEND YOUR SESSION LOG.

We use all of this only to find and fix problems in the game. The lawful basis is our legitimate interest in making the game work (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)). Apple does not let us share it, and we don’t. Feedback and crash reports stay in Apple’s App Store Connect, where only our team can see them, and we delete them no later than twelve months after the beta ends. A session log you email us is kept like any other email.

If you write to us

When you email support@cloudberrix.com we receive your email address, what you write, and any device or game detail you include. We use it to answer you, or to fix what you reported, and for nothing else. The lawful basis is our legitimate interest in supporting the game (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)), or performance of a contract where your message concerns a purchase.

We keep correspondence while the issue is open and no longer than two years after it is closed, then delete it. Writing to us does not put you on a mailing list, and we never sell your address or share it with anyone except the provider that carries our email.

That provider is Google, which hosts our email (Google Workspace) and handles messages only on our instructions, under a data processing agreement. Google may process them outside the European Economic Area. Where it does, the transfer is covered by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and the European Commission’s standard contractual clauses.

Beta and launch news

If you gave us your email address to hear about the BONKERS beta or its launch, we use it to send you the beta invitation and news about the game, and for nothing else. The lawful basis is your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)). You can take it back at any time by replying to any of our emails or writing to support@cloudberrix.com, and we remove your address. We keep the list with our email at Google, as above, and delete it when we stop sending BONKERS news. We never sell it, share it, or use it for advertising.

Children

BONKERS is made for a general audience. It collects no personal data from anyone who plays it, children included. Purchases go through Apple, where a parent who uses Ask to Buy in Family Sharing approves each one first.

Signing up for beta or launch news is for people aged 13 and over. A parent or guardian can sign up for a younger child. If we learn that we hold a child’s email address without a parent’s permission, we delete it. A parent can ask at any time what we hold about their child and have it deleted.

Deleting your data

  • On your phone. Deleting the app deletes everything it kept on the phone.
  • In iCloud. Turn CLOUD SAVE off in SETTINGS › ACCOUNT, or turn iCloud off for BONKERS in the Settings app, and the game stops writing to your iCloud. The copy already there holds only your progress, settings and scoreboard names, and only BONKERS, on a phone signed in to your Apple Account, can read it.
  • Game Center. Sign out, or choose who can see your profile, in the Settings app under Game Center. Your Game Center data is held by Apple, and requests about it go to Apple, for example through privacy.apple.com.
  • Your emails to us, the news list and beta feedback. Ask at support@cloudberrix.com and we will delete them. To leave the beta, tap Stop Testing in TestFlight or delete the app.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask for access to the personal data we hold about you, have it corrected or erased, restrict or object to how we use it, and receive it in a portable form. Where we rely on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time; that does not affect what we did before. Since all we ever hold is what you sent us, your place on the news list and beta feedback, these requests are usually resolved the same week. Email support@cloudberrix.com and we will answer within one month, as the regulation requires. Exercising these rights is free.

You never have to give us personal data: the game works the same without it. We make no automated decisions about you and build no profiles.

In the United States. We do not sell or share personal information, as California law defines those terms, and we never have. The rights above are open to you wherever you live.

If you believe we have handled your data improperly you may complain to a supervisory authority: in our case the Estonian Data Protection Inspectorate (aki.ee), or the authority where you live. We would rather you told us first so we can put it right.

Changes

If this notice changes we will update the effective date above. If a future version of BONKERS processes data in a way this page does not already describe, we will say so here, and its App Store privacy label will reflect it, before that version ships.
