Help with an app

Something not working?

Tell us what you expected to happen and what happened instead. If you can, include your device, your iOS version, and the app version from its settings screen — it usually turns a week of guessing into a same-day fix.

Ideas

Something missing?

Feature requests genuinely get read, and a fair few of them get built. Tell us the problem you’re trying to solve rather than the solution you have in mind — it gives us more room to solve it well.

Purchases

Billing and refunds

Apple sells our apps and handles every payment, so refunds are requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. Lost an unlock after changing device? Use Restore Purchases in the app — and if that fails, write to us.

support@cloudberrix.com
