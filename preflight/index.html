Preflight Kit.

iPhone · iOS 17 and later

Our first app. Twelve flight-computer tools for student pilots, each one on a single screen, and every one of them works with no signal.

Why we built it

Most of learning to fly happens on the ground.

Preflight Kit is for the kitchen table the night before the lesson. Change one number and the drawing changes with it. That is the part a whizz-wheel cannot do: show you what a number means while you are still learning what it is.
