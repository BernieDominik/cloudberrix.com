Why we built it

Most of learning to fly happens on the ground.

Preflight Kit is for the kitchen table the night before the lesson. Work a heading, a fuel figure or a crosswind as many times as it takes for it to stop being hard. No exercise sheet, no timer, no marking.

Change one number and the drawing changes with it. Swing the wind round the compass and the drift changes sides. Push the crosswind past the limit and the verdict turns red. That is the part a whizz-wheel cannot do: show you what a number means while you are still learning what it is.

The kit

Twelve modules.

Every one is a single screen with the working shown, grouped the way a flight is: what you do at the table, what the wind is doing, and what you need with the aeroplane already moving. Nothing is buried in a menu, because in the air you have no spare hand for menus.

Plan at the table

PLOGHeadings, times and legs for the whole route.
FuelThe minimum you must carry, and the margin you actually have.
Takeoff & landingYour POH figure taken through the CAA safety factors, with a verdict against the runway you have.
Climb & descentTop of climb, top of descent, and the rate to fly.

Wind & air before you go

Wind StarDrift and groundspeed for every track, on one dial.
Wind FinderThe wind’s speed and direction, read back from the drift you are seeing.
CrosswindThe head and cross split, against the limit.
Air & altitudesPressure altitude, density altitude and TAS.

In the air on the kneeboard

Time · Speed · DistanceAny two of them give the third.
DiversionNew heading and new time, in seconds.
Back on trackThe 1-in-60 correction, drawn.
Holding patternEntry sector, drift headings and the outbound time.
And the small things

Kilograms to pounds, litres to gallons, feet to metres, knots, Celsius, hectopascals. Drag to scrub, no keyboard. Fuel reserve rules you set once, day and night. Haptics on every verdict.

The aeroplane

The aeroplane your school flies.

Pick the type and its book figures arrive already entered, checked against the POH. Add the registration and the empty weight off the school’s schedule, and it is ready before your next lesson.
