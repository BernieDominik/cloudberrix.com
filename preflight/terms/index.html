Aircraft templates

The aircraft templates included with Preflight Kit are transcriptions of figures published in manufacturers' handbooks, for one aircraft type and one handbook revision. Your aircraft may differ — a different revision, different equipment, a different weighing schedule, a different empty weight. Treat a template as a convenience for setting up and check every figure against the handbook and weighing schedule for your own aircraft before you use it.
